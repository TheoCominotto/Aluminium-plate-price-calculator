Beam pricing

The two smallest dimensions determine the beam section; the largest dimension is its length. Each beam is charged by length plus a fixed cutting cost. Unlisted sections are shown as unpriced and excluded from the total.

Combined stock bars

Plates with the same height (thickness) are grouped by exact matching width or length. The lower-cost orientation is used for each thickness. Kerf is added between adjacent parts and backup length to each bar. Plates with no orientation under the maximum cutting width are priced individually and flagged over-limit.
